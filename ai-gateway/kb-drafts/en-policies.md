## Document: Refund Policy
Refunds are reviewed by our support team; they are never issued automatically.
To request a refund, tell the assistant which ride or payment is affected and why.
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within [CONFIRM: 5] business days.
Refunds to the wallet are usually instant once approved. [CONFIRM]
A refund may be declined if the ride was completed as booked and no fault is found.

## Document: Cancellation Policy
You can cancel a ride from the app or by asking the assistant, which will ask you to confirm first.
Cancelling before a driver accepts is free.
Cancelling after a driver has accepted may incur a cancellation fee of [CONFIRM: amount in DZD].
